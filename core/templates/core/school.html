Career Gateway for schools

Tools for school students and teachers to explore careers early. Everything below is a way to explore and ask for help. This page does not book sessions or seats; registering only records your interest for the school team.

What students can do

  • Career Assistant: answer a few questions and see career ideas with the reasons shown (an exploration activity, not a test of suitability).
  • Explore careers: read what professionals do and what is needed.
  • Mentors: see working professionals and send a request.
  • Opportunities: programs, competitions and internships to look at.
  • Request a counsellor: ask a person for help with personal decisions.
For students under 18: we keep this simple. Registering for a program asks only for your school name and a parent or guardian consent. We take no phone number, email or address for school programs, and the staff dashboard shows only counts.
