How Career Gateway works

Career Gateway puts three kinds of help in order: an AI assistant for access, human counsellors for guidance, and working professionals for real experience. The assistant does not replace a professional counsellor.

AI: understand, inform, explore, connect

Answers common questions, shows career ideas with the reasons, and points to the right page or person.

Counsellor: assess, discuss, guide, plan

Helps with personal decisions, college comparison and admissions. The assistant suggests this when a question needs judgement.

Mentor: real experience

A working professional explains what the job is really like and sets realistic expectations.

How a question is answered

  1. Retrieve. The question is matched against published records (careers, colleges, opportunities, mentors, FAQs, entrance exams and more). Each record carries a verified or not verified label and a fictional demo label where it applies.
  2. Generate. A free language model (Google Gemini free tier, or a local Ollama model) writes a short answer using only those records, with source numbers like [1].
  3. Check yourself. Every record used is listed under the answer, so you can open it and confirm.
  4. Fall back. If nothing is found, or the model is busy, we show the records or say we could not find it. We do not guess.

How matching works

  • Colleges: hard limits (such as eligibility and budget) exclude a college with the reason shown. Preferences only change the order. Missing information is flagged, never silently ignored.
  • Partner colleges are labelled clearly, and being a partner never changes the order.
  • Handoff: situations such as a college comparison, an admission decision or confusion between options suggest a human counsellor.
  • Leads: requests are recorded for the team. The system does not book sessions or take payments.

Honest limits

  • Search is word-based. It does not yet use embeddings or a vector database.
  • Colleges, opportunities and mentors shown here are fictional demo data, and knowledge entries are not verified yet.
  • The assistant does not replace a counsellor, and a written answer can still contain mistakes.
  • Free AI services may use submitted question text to improve their products, so please do not type personal details.
